Setup Guide

Getting Isele running on your laptop

A one-time setup, then it's a single double-click whenever you want it. No installing, no settings — just follow these steps in order. Takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • The download link and your password — both sent to you separately.
  • Your laptop plugged in and on Wi-Fi (the download is large).
  • About 15 minutes for the first setup. After that, starting Isele takes seconds.
1

Download the file

Open the download link that was sent to you and save the file. It's called Isele.zip and it's big (about 5 GB), so give it a few minutes on good Wi-Fi. It usually lands in your Downloads folder.

2

Unzip it

Find Isele.zip, right-click it, and choose Extract All… → Extract. This unpacks it into a normal folder called Isele. (Leave the folder where it is — don't run it from inside the zip.)

3

Start Isele

Open the Isele folder and double-click START Isele. A black window will appear — that's normal, it's getting everything ready.

4

If you see a blue “Windows protected your PC” box

Windows shows this for anything not from its store. It's expected here — just click More info, then Run anyway.

5

If Windows asks about the firewall

You may get a “Windows Security Alert” asking to allow network access. Leave the boxes ticked and click Allow access. This lets Isele reach the internet.

6

Wait about a minute

The black window sets everything up and then your browser opens to Isele automatically. The first start is the slowest (it's loading the voice) — later starts are quick.

7

Log in and you're done

On the Isele screen, type the password that was sent to you and press Enter. That's it — you're in and ready to go.

Every day after this

To start

Open the Isele folder and double-click START Isele. No warnings this time.

To stop

Close the black window. That shuts everything down cleanly.

If something's not right

The browser didn't open on its own

Open your browser and go to isele.vercel.app yourself, then log in. The black window just needs to be running.

The voice says “offline” or won't connect

Give it a few more seconds after the black window says “Isele is running.” If it's still offline, close the black window and double-click START Isele again.

The video is slow or laggy

This depends on your internet speed, since the video is processed online. A stronger Wi-Fi connection helps most. Everything else can keep running normally.

Nothing happens when I double-click START Isele

Make sure you extracted the zip in step 2 and are opening the real Isele folder — not running it from inside the zip. Then try again.
